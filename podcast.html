Strangers on the Internet

Founder & co-host, since 2022

Irina Manta is the founder and co-host of Strangers on the Internet, a podcast exploring the people, scams, and legal questions that come out of life lived online — from dating and romance fraud to privacy, platform accountability, and the strange corners of digital life.

New and past episodes are available on all major podcast platforms, as well as at strangersoninternet.com and on Acast.
